Hi, I’m Suthikshan. This is what I noticed when I first used IncuBrix.

When I log in, the home page is simple. There’s a box where you type an idea, and templates for LinkedIn content, podcast content, video content and repurposing video. It’s clear what you can make.

I tried the LinkedIn Content template. I typed an idea about creators wasting time reformatting videos. It asked what kind of post I wanted, and I chose a single LinkedIn post. It wrote a full draft.

The first draft was longer than I asked for, so I asked for a rewrite: under 150 words, remove two claims that had no source, and end with a question. It followed all three. That editing worked well.

It also used 17 credits for the draft and the rewrite, from my 300. I like that the balance is visible at the top.

Some things confused me as a new user. The Content Hub was empty, and the publishing page said to upload files first, but it also had an Upload button. It also asked me to reconnect my social accounts when none were connected yet. The analytics page showed sample data, and it says so, which is good.

Also, TikTok isn’t available for my region, so I couldn’t connect it.

Overall, making a draft was quick, and editing it by chat is easy. The first few screens could be clearer for new users.

Thanks for watching.
