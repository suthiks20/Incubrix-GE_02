Resizing a video is not the same as reframing it.

A landscape video squeezed into a vertical frame leaves bars above and below. The viewer sees a small picture inside a big screen.

Reframing follows the speaker and fills the frame. It is one more step, and it is the step that makes a clip look made for the platform.

Do you reframe, or resize and hope?
