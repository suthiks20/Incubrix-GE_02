Posting one video everywhere is not the same as posting well.

Each platform has its own shape. LinkedIn rewards context. Reels and Shorts reward a fast first line and a vertical frame.

I keep the idea and change three things: the first line, the frame and the ending.

It is slower than copy and paste. It also does not look copied.

Which of the three do you change first?
